Blue Notice Deletion

Request the removal of an INTERPOL Blue Notice.
Not all Blue Notices are lawfully issued. You have a legal option. It is called "Deletion Request".

I am flagged by an INTERPOL Blue Notice. Do I have to worry?

Being subject to an INTERPOL Blue Notice is not that serious as being subject to a Red Notice. 

However, it is a serious legal matter that should not be ignored.

A Blue Notice is used by INTERPOL to locate a person or obtain additional information in the context of an ongoing investigation.

Although this does not automatically authorise arrest, it may lead to questioning, travel disruptions, or increased scrutiny by national authorities.

The level of risk depends on the specific circumstances of the case, including whether a criminal investigation is active.

The prudent course is to seek a legal assessment rather than assume it is harmless or panic unnecessarily.

Do you need an arrest warrant for an INTERPOL Blue Notice?

No. Unlike a Red Notice, a Blue Notice does not require a valid arrest warrant.

An INTERPOL Blue Notice is issued for the purpose of:

a) Locating a person,

b) Obtaining additional information about identity,

c) Gathering information related to an ongoing investigation.

However, although an arrest warrant is not mandatory, a criminal investigation must be ongoing at the national level. Without an active investigation, the notice may lack a sufficient legal basis and may be challengeable.

Can an INTERPOL Blue Notice be deleted?

Yes. An INTERPOL Blue Notice can be deleted if it does not comply with INTERPOL’s legal framework. Deletion is not automatic. It requires a formal request before the Commission for the Control of INTERPOL’s Files (“CCF”), which examines whether the notice meets the requirements of INTERPOL’s Constitution and the Rules on the Processing of Data (“RPD”).

How difficult is to delete a Blue Notice?

Deleting an INTERPOL Blue Notice is possible, but it is not simple. In general, deletion can be challenging because:

  • The Applicant carries the burden of proof.

  • The Commission does not reassess criminal guilt or innocence.

  • The review focuses strictly on compliance with INTERPOL’s rules and procedural standards.

Success typically depends on demonstrating that the notice does not meet INTERPOL’s admissibility criteria, lacks a sufficient legal basis, or breaches the Organization’s constitutional principles.

A carefully prepared submission supported by clear legal arguments and relevant documentation significantly improves the prospects of deletion.

How long does INTERPOL Blue Notice deletion take?

The deletion procedure is not immediate. 

In most cases, the admissibility phase takes approximately one month. During this stage, the CCF examines whether the request meets the formal requirements. Once the application is declared admissible, the substantive review begins. The merits phase, which assesses whether the Red Notice complies with INTERPOL’s Constitution and Rules on the Processing of Data, typically takes around nine months.

The overall timeframe may vary depending on the complexity of the case. 

Can political motivation lead to the deletion of a Blue Notice?

Yes. Under Article 3 of INTERPOL’s Constitution is strictly prohibited from undertaking activities of a political character.

The Commission applies the “predominance test”, examining:

  • The nature of the offence,

  • The status of the individual,

  • The general context of the case,

  • The sufficiency of evidence linking the person to the alleged crime.

If political elements outweigh ordinary-law aspects, the notice must be deleted.

Immediate strategic action is essential. Travel should be suspended until the risk is assessed. The underlying judicial documentation must be analysed, and the extradition exposure evaluated by the jurisdiction. A structured Deletion Request should then be filed before the Commission for the Control of INTERPOL’s Files. 

Do I need a lawyer to delete an INTERPOL Red Notice?

While it is technically possible to file a request independently, Blue Notice deletion is a specialised field involving international police cooperation law, extradition principles, and human rights analysis.

A lawyer experienced in INTERPOL matters can assess violations of INTERPOL’s framework
and identify procedural deficiencies (at national level).

Improper submissions often result in delay or rejection, with any opportunity for appeal. Strategic preparation significantly increases the likelihood of success.

What are the chances of deleting an INTERPOL Red Notice?

The success rate depends entirely on the legal grounds of the case. Red Notices are frequently deleted where there is clear evidence of political motivation, civil-commercial disputes disguised as criminal matters, refugee status, or lack of due process guarantees.

Cases involving genuine, serious criminal convictions with proper judicial basis are more complex. Each case requires individual legal assessment. A preliminary legal review is essential to determine viability.

Will deleting a Red Notice remove my data completely?

If the CCF orders deletion, the data are removed from INTERPOL’s systems. Member states are instructed to delete the data from their national databases to the extent derived from INTERPOL channels.

However, separate domestic arrest warrants or parallel bilateral alerts may still exist. A comprehensive strategy should address both INTERPOL and national exposure.

Can I travel while my Red Notice deletion request is pending?

Travelling while subject to a Red Notice carries risk. Some jurisdictions act immediately on INTERPOL alerts, while others require domestic judicial validation. Unless the Red Notice blocking was ordered, it is not safe to travel abroad. 

What are the benefits of deleting an INTERPOL Red Notice?

The benefits of INTERPOL Red Notice deletion include restored freedom of movement, reduced risk of international arrest, improved banking compliance outcomes, and enhanced immigration prospects. For entrepreneurs, executives, and internationally active professionals, deleting a Red Notice can be decisive in restoring business operations and cross-border transactions.

In addition, a successful deletion confirms that the Red Notice violated INTERPOL’s legal framework. This may be due to political motivation, or human rights violations in the requesting country. Such findings can significantly strengthen parallel extradition defence and domestic legal proceedings.

Why is it dangerous to leave a Red Notice unchallenged?

An active Red Notice remains visible in international law enforcement systems. Even if you are not immediately detained, the notice may be triggered at any time during border checks, visa applications, financial compliance reviews, or corporate due diligence procedures.

Unchallenged Red Notices can be used as tools of political or commercial pressure. Deletion is often the only effective mechanism to eliminate ongoing international exposure.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • 1. What is an Interpol Blue Notice?

    An INTERPOL Blue Notice is an international request circulated through INTERPOL to collect information about a person’s identity, location, or activities in connection with a criminal investigation. It is not an arrest warrant and should not be used to justify detention or restrictive measures.

  • 2. Can an INTERPOL Blue Notice be deleted?

    Yes. A person may request deletion of an INTERPOL Blue Notice before the Commission for the Control of INTERPOL’s Files if the notice violates INTERPOL’s rules, (e.g. lacks sufficient legal basis, no longer serves a legitimate purpose).

  • 3. Is a Blue Notice the same as a Red Notice?

    No. A Red Notice seeks the location and provisional arrest of a person wanted for prosecution or sentencing. A Blue Notice is used to obtain information or locate a person of interest and does not authorise arrest, as it is not based on a national arrest warrant.

  • 4. Can a Blue Notice affect international travel?

    Yes. Although Blue Notices are formally non-restrictive, they may lead to questioning, border delays, enhanced scrutiny, visa issues, or refusal of entry if national authorities act upon the information circulated through INTERPOL.

  • 5. Can association with a group justify a Blue Notice?

    Association alone should not justify a Blue Notice unless there is specific, reliable information linking the person to identifiable criminal conduct. A notice based mainly on alleged affiliation may be vulnerable to challenge to the Commission.

In INTERPOL proceedings, the first submission is often the only real opportunity. A single procedural or legal error can lead to long-term or permanent consequences. For this reason, Red Notice cases should never be treated as administrative formalities. They require specialised legal expertise, precision, and strategy from the very beginning.
